# Arko identity study

Three original object directions were considered before selecting the first implementation:

1. **Tension Seal (selected):** an asymmetric aubergine ceramic clasp holding three amber folded ribbons, with a small jade pressure stud. The negative space and offset ribbon ends make a recognizable silhouette. Compacting and holding are conveyed without a box, folder, zipper, or animal.
2. **Concertina Stone:** a pale, smooth river-stone shell opening onto tightly pleated vermilion layers. Strong tactile character, but the closed silhouette loses legibility at 16 px.
3. **Counterweight:** two ivory half-discs held together by a single brass counterweight, containing a dark woven filament. Distinctive industrial character, but more mechanical and less immediately associated with stored layers.

`scripts/render-icon.swift` contains original resolution-independent Tension Seal artwork, producing the standard macOS iconset including 16/32/64/128/256/512/1024 pixel rasters. `iconutil` packages it in the app. No Keka shapes, animal, pose, colors, or composition are reused. This is an original first identity, not an SF Symbol placeholder.

Visual approval at actual Dock/Finder sizes and a coherent document-icon family remain a real-Mac follow-up. Do not call that validation complete merely because raster generation succeeds.
